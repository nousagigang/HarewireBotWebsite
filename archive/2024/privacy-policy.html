Privacy Policy

Harewire Bot
This policy applies to all Discord servers where the Harewire bot is installed.
Last date updated: 25 January 2026
1. DATA COLLECTION

Harewire collects only the data necessary for its features to function. This includes:

  • User IDs and Usernames: To identify users for commands and roles.
  • Server IDs: To store settings specific to each server.
  • Interaction Data: Temporary logs of commands used to ensure the bot is working correctly.
2. DATA STORAGE & THIRD PARTIES

Harewire is built using BotGhost. Therefore, all data is stored and processed on BotGhost’s secure servers and through the Discord API. We do not use any external databases or sell data to third parties.
